02

The video: "The World's Best Everyday Jewelry"

Reworked to match Ellie's own pitch from the call: real, overlooked moments pulled from the same morning routine across several different days, so the jewelry, not the outfit, is the one constant. The wardrobe changes cut together the way Andy's city-walk transformation does in The Devil Wears Prada: quick, seamless, and never the point of the shot.

Wardrobe options

Each of the nine looks below is meant to be featured as a look for each different morning of the same routine. Cut together beat to beat, the outfit changes with every cut while the routine, and the jewelry, stays identical.

Outfit 1

Beats

Each cut below carries her into a new outfit and a new day, edited seamlessly, the same trick as the wardrobe-change montage in The Devil Wears Prada.

Beat 1
She stops at the mirror to put in her Comfort Studs. The first calm beat of the video, and the first clear shot of the jewelry.
Beat 2
Down into the subway, foot tapping on the platform. A second calm beat lands on the earring while everything else keeps moving.
Beat 3
In line for coffee, same restless energy. Another quiet beat on the jewelry before she's back in motion.
Beat 4
She catches her reflection, or her phone, once more before walking into the meeting. Same earrings, same routine, new day.

Closing line

"Made for everyday moments, Jane," exactly as Ellie described it on the call.
