02

The video: "The World's Best Everyday Jewelry"

One continuous day, told through quick match-cut transitions that always resolve on her ear or face, so the Comfort Studs stay the visual anchor throughout.

Beats

Beat 1
She hails a cab on the street. Quick cut to her ducking her head into the backseat: the close-up lands here, ear catching the light as she lowers in.
Beat 2
She pushes her sunglasses up from the bridge of her nose to the top of her head. The motion naturally brushes her hair back and reveals the earring in the same gesture.
Beat 3
Open, to be locked with the team: a call answered with a hand tucking hair behind the ear is one option, kept in the same "on the move" register as the first two.

Closing line

"The world's best everyday jewelry," Jane's own trademarked tagline, exactly as written.

  • Runtime: approximately 20 seconds, with an optional 10-second cutdown
  • Formats: vertical and square, delivered as 1080p MP4
  • Reference footage on hand: a Comfort Studs lifestyle clip and a Comfort Studs product clip
  • Revisions: up to two rounds per the signed agreement
Open item: Ellie's own "Everyday Moments" concept (real day-long shoot, alarm to subway to coffee to the meeting, cut into a hero video plus six-second moments) covers similar ground with a different production approach and a different closing line ("made for everyday moments, Jane"). Worth confirming which version, or which pieces of each, before this goes into production.
